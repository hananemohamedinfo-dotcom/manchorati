Manchorati • Privacy Policy & Terms

Compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Google AdSense Policies & Google Play Developer Policies

Effective Date & Last Updated: November 2026

1. Introduction & Acceptance

Welcome to Manchorati. We are firmly committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security and transparent processing of your personal information in accordance with international data protection laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Google Play Developer Policies.

By using or installing Manchorati, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

  • Account & Authentication: When you sign in via Google or email, we collect your display name, email address, profile picture URL, and unique User ID (UID) to authenticate, secure, and personalize your account.
  • User-Generated Content (UGC): Quotes, designed cards, posts, comments, likes, and bookmarks that you create, securely hosted on Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Media & Images: Profile avatars and background images you voluntarily upload for card designs, encrypted and stored on Google Firebase Storage.
  • AI Writing Assistant: Prompts submitted to the in-app AI Assistant (powered by Google Gemini) are processed transiently solely to generate requested text and are neither sold nor used for proprietary model training.
  • Technical Identifiers & Notifications: Anonymous Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens for delivery of push notifications (e.g. daily inspirational quote, interaction alerts), as well as locally stored preferences (language, dark theme).

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Art. 6)

We process your personal information under the following legal bases:

  • Consent: You give explicit consent upon signing up and agreeing to these terms.
  • Contractual Necessity: To deliver core services, including publishing, social interactions, and card generation.
  • Legitimate Interests: To maintain application security, combat spam, and enforce community safety.

4. Data Security & Encryption

All network traffic and data exchange between the application and backend cloud servers are strictly encrypted in transit using HTTPS and TLS 1.3 protocols. Cleartext (unencrypted) transmission is blocked entirely. Rigorous Firebase Security Rules prevent unauthorized data access.

5. Instant In-App Account & Data Deletion

Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): In full adherence to Google Play Data Safety requirements and GDPR Article 17, Manchorati provides an instant in-app account deletion mechanism. Users can permanently delete their account and all associated data directly from their profile menu (with a 5-second safety confirmation countdown).

Upon confirmation, authentication credentials, user profiles, social connections, and stored data are permanently and irreversibly purged from our servers.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

  • Right of Access & Rectification: You can review and update your profile details and posts directly within the app.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may disable push notifications or log out at any time.
  • Right to Erasure: Delete your account instantly via the in-app option or contact our team for assistance.

7. Cookies, Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

Manchorati uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance user navigation, analyze web traffic, and display relevant, high-quality advertisements via trusted partners, specifically Google AdSense:

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on Manchorati.
  • Google's use of the DART cookie enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of the use of the DART cookie for interest-based advertising by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy.
  • We provide an interactive Cookie Consent Banner allowing users to accept or decline non-essential cookies at any time in full compliance with GDPR and CCPA regulations.

8. Community Standards & Content Moderation

Hate speech, harassment, discrimination, graphic violence, adult content, and copyright infringement are strictly prohibited. The administration reserves the right to immediately remove offending material and permanently ban responsible accounts.

9. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Manchorati is intended for individuals aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Contact & Official Inquiries

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or to exercise your privacy rights, please reach us via email at support@manchorati.com or via our Contact Us page.
